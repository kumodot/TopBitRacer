TopBitRacer v0.4.3

A 1-bit pseudo-3D racer made for a 128x64 SSD1306 OLED: tune your car in the garage, race three cups, outrun the police.

W S gas / brake, ← → steer, E nitro, Enter start / pause, M music on / off.
