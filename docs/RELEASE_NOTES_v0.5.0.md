## TopBitRacer v0.5.0

Same layouts, different races.

- **Conditions change every race**: day, dusk or night; clear, fog, rain, storm (or snow on FROST PEAK); dirt sections move around; puddles appear on wet roads.
- **Forecast and weather changes**: the track screen shows the conditions and the forecast (*LATER: RAIN?*) before the garage, so you can pick tires. Rain can start, skies can clear, fog can roll in during the race. Grip follows the road as it soaks and dries, for you and the rivals.
- **Puddles**: drag, a twitch of the wheel and a spray of water.

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.5.0.html` with a double click.
