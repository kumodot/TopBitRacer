## TopBitRacer v0.4.1

- **Payout screen**: prize and fines count up after each career race, then the money rolls into your bank with a payday jingle. Press A to skip.
- **Cup bonus for the top 3**: Rookie $1500 / $700 / $300, Pro $5000 / $2500 / $1000, Elite $15000 / $7000 / $3000. Only the winner unlocks the next cup.

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.4.1.html` with a double click.
