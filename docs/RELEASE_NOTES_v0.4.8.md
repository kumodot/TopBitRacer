## TopBitRacer v0.4.8

- **Wanted level**: during a police chase, 1 to 3 stars in the top right corner, with the fine you'd pay if caught right now.
  - 1 star: speeding. Stop when told and you pay the minimum.
  - 2 stars: fleeing (ignoring the order, nitro, slingshot or cutting past the cop).
  - 3 stars: hitting the cop or making it ram you. Maximum fine.
  - Escape and you pay nothing.
- Fines per star: Rookie $100 / $250 / $500, Pro $300 / $700 / $1200, Elite $800 / $1500 / $2500.

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.4.8.html` with a double click.
