## TopBitRacer v0.4.4

- **Dynamic race music**: the faster and wilder you drive, the more the song opens up. Speed, nitro, slingshot, slipstream, police chases and the final lap push the tempo up a bit and add layers (arpeggios, driving bass, ghost hats). Crashes calm it down for a few seconds; a wreck strips it back to drums and bass.
- Layers change on the beat or bar, so it always stays in time. Menu music plays as written.
- Includes v0.4.3 (quieter pause, resume countdown).

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.4.4.html` with a double click.
