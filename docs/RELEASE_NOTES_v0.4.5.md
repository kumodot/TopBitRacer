## TopBitRacer v0.4.5

- **Grid fix**: rivals no longer slide sideways before GO.
- **Closer racing**: rivals have a wider spread of pace and launch, and a smooth catch-up: when you lead, the front runners push to reel you in; a rival far ahead eases off.
- **Police balance**: the cop is a bit slower, starts further back, loses you after a short time out of sight (faster on nitro) and gives up after 16 s. In *STOP NOW* mode you can dodge it. You can also draft behind the police car.
- **FIRST!** flashes with a jingle when you take the lead.

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.4.5.html` with a double click.
