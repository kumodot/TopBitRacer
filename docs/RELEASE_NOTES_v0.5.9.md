## TopBitRacer v0.5.9

- **Full screen**: just the game screen in its frame, sharp and big. Great on a Steam Deck. Use the *Full screen* button, **V**, a double-click on the screen, the gamepad **View** button or **SCREEN** in the game menu. Tick *Auto* to go full screen on your first key press or tap.
- Includes v0.5.8 (gamepad fixes, manual gears).

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.5.9.html` with a double click.
