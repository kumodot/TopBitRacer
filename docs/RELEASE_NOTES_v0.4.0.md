## TopBitRacer v0.4.0

- **Burnout style nitro**: *NEAR MISS* gives a quarter charge, drafting slowly refills it.
- **Speed lines** you can actually see while drafting, slingshotting and on nitro.
- **Police**: traps are manned only some laps and don't always chase; the cop car now collides with rivals and drives around traffic.
- **Arcade obstacles**: bushes and signs fly away, trees throw you back to the road, buildings nearly stop you.
- **Overtaking**: wider road and a slightly smaller car hitbox.
- Includes v0.3.3 (cleaner screen, pit lane, dirt sections).

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.4.0.html` with a double click.
