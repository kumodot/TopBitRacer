## TopBitRacer v0.5.8

- **Gamepad fixes** (tested on Steam Deck): pulling the stick back no longer brakes, so steering can't brake by accident (brake is LT / B). Nitro moves to **A** (also Y), gas on **RT** (also X).
- **Manual gears** (option in the main menu: GEARS AUTO / MANUAL): **R / F** on the keyboard, **RB / LB** on a gamepad. Shift at the right moment for a little extra pull; too high a gear bogs, each gear has a rev limiter, downshifts engine-brake.
- Includes v0.5.7 (rivals answer your nitro, real catch-up, cup contenders).

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.5.8.html` with a double click.
