## TopBitRacer v0.5.3

- **Nose-up animation**: off a ramp the car tilts half way, then fully up, seen almost from above with the headlights, hood and roof showing, then levels out at the peak.
- **Speed lines** burst out on takeoff and streak off the bottom of the car on the way up.
- Includes v0.5.1 (jump ramps, potholes, birds).

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.5.3.html` with a double click.
