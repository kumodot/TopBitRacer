## TopBitRacer v0.5.1

- **Jump ramps**: about every other race a straight gets a broken stretch of road with a ramp in one lane. Warning signs and painted arrows show the lane. Hit it and you fly: the camera rides up with the car, the horizon dips, the engine revs free and the music fades, then slams back in on landing.
- **Suspension matters**: high lands soft with a little hop; low slams down and fishtails.
- **Potholes**: miss the ramp (or land short) and the potholes eat your speed; high suspension and rally tires lose the least.
- **Rivals jump too** (the better drivers more often). The police don't.
- **Birds**: one sometimes crosses your flight path. *BIRD STRIKE!*
- Includes v0.5.0 (conditions that change every race).

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.5.1.html` with a double click.
