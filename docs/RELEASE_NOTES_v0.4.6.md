## TopBitRacer v0.4.6

- **Rivals that race**: they brake before corners, take the inside line and obey the same grip rules as you. Too fast into a bend and they slide wide, more on snow and rain.
- **Personalities**: every rival has its own skill (racing line, how well it reads the grip) and aggression (only some defend their line, never mid-corner).
- Includes v0.4.5 (grid fix, closer racing, police balance, FIRST! flash).

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.4.6.html` with a double click.
