## TopBitRacer v0.5.4

- **New nose-up frame for jumps**: the roof becomes the front with headlights, a wide windshield with mirrors, and the rear squashed down with smaller tail lights and wheels. Shown from takeoff to the peak of the jump.
- Includes v0.5.3 (takeoff speed lines) and v0.5.1 (jump ramps, potholes, birds).

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.5.4.html` with a double click.
