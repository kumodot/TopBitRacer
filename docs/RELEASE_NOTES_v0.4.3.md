## TopBitRacer v0.4.3

- **Quieter pause**: the music drops to 10% in the pause menu.
- **Resume countdown**: back from pause you get 3-2-1-GO with the world frozen. Rev the engine while you wait; the music fades back in on GO.

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.4.3.html` with a double click.
