## TopBitRacer v0.5.7

- **Rivals answer your nitro**: fire one and the rivals close by fire theirs after a short reaction.
- **Real catch-up**: when you lead, the front runners can reel you in even when you're flat out on the straights. The mid-pack doesn't, so it's a race against two or three, not a wall.
- **Contenders**: the two rivals leading the cup start on the front row, drive more cleanly and get an extra nitro plus a fresh charge every lap.
- Includes v0.5.6 (rarer rain and fog).

Play online at https://kumodot.github.io/TopBitRacer/ or open `TopBitRacer_v0.5.7.html` with a double click.
